# All-feature review — 2026-10-14

Scope: current implemented workflows and the original 18-phase roadmap. This is a local backend/integration, source, build and dependency review, not a claim that every planned feature or every browser interaction is complete. Existing backups, environment files and real financial records were preserved. Prior completed export work was already committed before this review.

## Findings fixed

1. Vendor price comparison previously compared different units and every historical quote. A low box price, superseded quotation, future quote or inactive vendor could become the displayed best price. Summary now compares the material's unit (case-insensitive, no automatic unit conversion), the latest effective quote per active vendor as of the Bangladesh business date, with deterministic ID tie-breaking. The original price history table remains available. The UI labels the basis and comparable vendor count, and reminds users to consider minimum quantity and lead time.
2. Price create/edit previously let malformed numbers and negative quantities/lead times reach the database. Both routes now reject invalid IDs, non-finite/non-positive prices, excess decimal places, negative/fractional lead times, impossible dates and invalid text. Numeric fields retain the database's two-decimal precision.
3. Dependency audit initially reported 13 vulnerable packages, including one critical advisory. Non-breaking dependency updates remove the critical proxy-addr issue and update qs/fast-uri, brace-expansion 5.0.12 and the direct MariaDB connector. Prisma and client generation are now 7.10.0. The adapter's pinned old driver is overridden to patched MariaDB 3.5.4, with full integration regression. Remaining advisories are listed below; no forced major downgrade to Prisma 6/nodemon 1 was used.
4. Roadmap's payroll row incorrectly still listed approvals/action audit as wholly unimplemented. It now reflects the existing completed work and the remaining allocation approval/refund scope.
